          Permanent places (OpenStreetMap)

          —

          Places and surveillance cameras come from a full copy of OpenStreetMap (Geofabrik, about 10 GB for the U.S.), downloaded once a month then sorted on the server with osmium: a plain file download, no API. Places corrected or added here are kept across updates; a hidden place stays hidden.

                            Flood of fake reports?

                            —

                            During the pause, the map stays visible but nobody can report. Organizers can still post announcements.

                            Erase reports

                            Everywhere, immediately. Erased points stay counted in the digest as “erased (purge)”.

                            Emergency wipe: every trace

                            Use this if a seizure of the server is coming. Erases at once: reports, announcements, organizer log, trash, anti-abuse fingerprints (including those of calendar and order suggestions), abuse log, notification subscriptions and the digest archive. A new hashing key is created: old fingerprints become unusable. Organizers, the public calendar and published orders are kept.

                            What the wipe can’t reach: the host’s backups and snapshots, copies already made elsewhere (browsers, screenshots), and the disk itself (a software wipe is not a physical wipe). If several processes are running (Passenger), stop them afterwards.
